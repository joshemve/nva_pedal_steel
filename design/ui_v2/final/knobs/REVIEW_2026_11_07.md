# Knob match review — unresolved

Joshua rejected the earlier studio rotation candidate as insufficiently accurate.
The new work has **not** achieved the requested reference match. No candidate in
this review is approved, and this work has not replaced the production strips.

## What was tested

- Native Cycles studies 24–46: dish, shoulder, cap curvature, circular finger
  recesses, axial grooves, collar geometry, pointer proportions and fixed lights.
- A direct native-render fitting loop over 19 global geometry/material/light
  parameters, followed by a shoulder-constrained 20-parameter fit.
- Additional fitted normal and lighting-response experiments. These were rejected
  for posterized highlights, poor rotation, or implausible surface shading.
- The Voice study adds a raised black collar and more visible skirt ribs. The
  Space study corrects the pointer's inner endpoint and vertical registration.

## Remaining failure

The Feel reference combines a broad face, sharply defined inner highlight arc,
rounded finger grooves and a more circular upper silhouette. The physical
rebuild still reads as a softly scalloped cap. Lower pixel error is insufficient:
unconstrained fits tend to remove the distinctive grooves. The edge-weighted fit
also fails the visual comparison. Do not mark the knobs finished based on these
fits or on center-color error alone.

The first direct-fit run incorrectly applied an extra sRGB transform when reading
PNG pixels through Blender. A byte-buffer test against Pillow established that
these values were already display-encoded. That run is preserved under
`cycles_fit/incorrect_gamma_run/`; the corrected run is `fit_corrected.log`.
Its scores must not be compared to the failed run's scores.

Subdivision studies 27–29 stalled inside Cycles and were terminated by their
specific process IDs. Unrelated Blender jobs were not terminated. All subsequent
studies use explicit geometry without that subdivision modifier. Other project
and production-file changes visible in the workspace are concurrent work and
were not reset or claimed as part of this review.

The native motion proof uses real rotating geometry under stationary illumination,
without a reference-color texture or a rotating baked highlight. That verifies
the animation mechanism, **not** pixel-perfect appearance or acceptance.
