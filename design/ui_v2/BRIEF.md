# Pedal Steel UI v2 — design brief

Joshua's picks (2026-11-06) from 648 Codex concepts:

- **Ref 1 — layout (#353, `ref/ref1_layout_353.png`)**: the base. Instrument across the top with the steel bar on the strings, a black control panel below grouped VOICE (Tone, Pick) / FEEL (Glide, Swell) / SPACE (Echo, Reverb), PLAY / MOTION / EFFECTS tabs, bottom strip with MODE menu, A B C LKL LKR, VOLUME PEDAL slider, MIDI LEARN, output meter and trim.
- **Ref 2 — quality and type (#378, `ref/ref2_brand_378.png`)**: take its script "Pedal Steel" wordmark with the triple burnt-orange stripe, the amber LED dot-matrix preset display ("SWEET GEORGIA E9 / 01 FACTORY"), the cream, walnut and burnt-orange palette and its clean small-caps labels.

## Fix list (Joshua's words)

1. "the buttons for ABC on 1 need improved": A, B, C are foot pedals and LKL, LKR are knee levers. Show them as real hardware: three chrome-and-rubber foot pedals (A B C) and two knee-lever paddles (LKL, LKR), each with an amber LED that lights when engaged (A and B engaged in the shot). No beige square keycaps.
2. "the pedal steel text": replace the plain serif with Ref 2's script wordmark and orange stripes; NORTHERN VALLEY AUDIO in spaced small caps.
3. "the concept image in the center needs angled realistically": the instrument is a real E9 10-string pedal steel (single neck), shown in correct three-quarter perspective from the player's seat, camera about 25–35° above the deck: keyhead with 10 tuning keys at the left end, changer and end plate at the right end, 10 strings over a black fretboard with inlaid position markers, chrome bar resting on the strings, walnut body, chrome rails, real cast shadow on the panel behind. Physically plausible lighting, no floating parts.
4. "LED preset area": the preset display is the amber dot-matrix LED from Ref 2, with ◀ ▶, BROWSE and SAVE.

## Format

Plugin window, 1600 × 1000 (16:10), flat front-on screenshot of the UI (not a photo of a screen). Ultra-realistic materials: brushed and polished chrome, walnut with real grain, black anodized panel, cream bakelite knobs with knurling, soft studio light from upper left. Every label crisp and correctly spelled.
